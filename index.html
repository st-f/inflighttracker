In Flight Tracker — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

In Flight Tracker ("the app") is published by Stephane Schittly. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

Summary

  • The app has no accounts and no adverts.
  • Your location is used on your device to show where you are. The app does not send your location to us or to anyone else.
  • Your flights, recorded paths and settings are stored on your device.
  • The app contacts the internet only to refresh its list of flight routes, to look up a flight number it does not know, and to handle purchases through Google Play.
  • The barcode scanner uses a Google component that sends Google technical diagnostics about how the scanner performs. It does not send the image or the barcode contents.

Information the app uses

Location

With your permission, the app reads your device's GPS position to show your position on the map, calculate your flight's progress and record the path you have flown. This happens on your device. Location data is not transmitted off your device by the app.

While a flight is being tracked, the app continues to read your location in the background, shown by a notification, so the path keeps recording with the screen off. You can stop tracking at any time.

Flights and recorded paths

Flights you add and the paths the app records are stored in the app's storage on your device. If device backup is turned on in Android, this data may be included in your device's backup to your Google account, under Google's terms. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes it from your device.

Camera

If you choose to scan a boarding pass, the app uses the camera to read the barcode. The image is processed on your device and is not stored or transmitted. The barcode's flight details are used to add the flight.

Calendar

If you turn on calendar import, the app reads upcoming events in your device calendar to find flights. Calendar contents are processed on your device and are not transmitted.

When the app uses the internet

  • Flight route updates. The app downloads an open dataset of flight routes from GitHub. This request does not include your location, your flights or any identifier created by the app. As with any internet request, the server sees your IP address.
  • Flight number lookup. If you search for a flight number the app does not have, it asks a public route service (adsb.lol) for that flight number. The request contains the flight number only.
  • Purchases. The one-time unlock is sold through Google Play Billing. Google handles the payment. The app receives confirmation that the purchase exists; it does not receive your payment details.

Third-party components

  • Google ML Kit (barcode scanning). Google's ML Kit collects device information (such as model and OS version), app information (package name and version), a per-installation identifier, and performance and diagnostic data (such as latency and error codes), and sends it to Google over an encrypted connection for diagnostics and usage analytics. See Google's ML Kit terms and privacy information.
  • Google Play Billing and Google Play services. Used to sell and verify the one-time unlock. See Google's Privacy Policy.

What we do not do

We do not collect, sell or share your personal information. We do not run analytics or advertising in the app. We have no servers that receive your data.

Children

The app is not directed at children.

Your choices

  • You can deny or revoke the location, camera, calendar and notification permissions in Android settings. The map still works without location, but cannot show your position.
  • You can delete individual flights in the app, or remove all app data by clearing its storage or uninstalling it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at the same address with a new date.
